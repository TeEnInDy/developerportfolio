# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Recruiters/hiring managers and freelance/contract clients, browsing informally to evaluate the owner for both employment and paid project work. No single audience takes priority over the other.

## Product Purpose
A personal developer portfolio site. It exists to give visitors (hiring managers, potential clients) a credible view of the owner's skills and work, in service of both job opportunities and freelance engagements.

## Positioning
Undecided. No confirmed differentiator or mechanism has been established yet.

## Operating Context
Repo contains a Next.js (App Router, v16) application at `app/` that is currently unmodified `create-next-app` boilerplate. A separate, fully designed dark-themed single-page portfolio UI exists at `src/src/App.tsx` (a Vite/React prototype, apparently Figma-Make-generated) with a navbar, hero, bento-grid project showcase, skills section, and contact section. This prototype is a UI/structure reference only — none of its content is real.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- All content currently in `src/src/App.tsx` (project names/descriptions like "Luminary," "Flux," "Helix CLI," stats like "40+ teams served," "2B+ events/month," the name "Thanapat," and "Bangkok, Thailand" location) is confirmed **fabricated placeholder** and must not be treated as evidence — it was generated, not supplied by the user.
- Real projects, work history, and skills to showcase are **undecided** — the user has not provided them yet. Do not invent replacements; carry this as an explicit open gap until supplied.
- Relationship between the Next.js app (`app/`) and the Vite prototype (`src/`) is unresolved: whether the prototype's design gets ported into the Next.js app, or the two are otherwise reconciled, is a build-time decision, not yet made.

## Evidence on Hand
None. No real projects, employers, testimonials, metrics, or bio content have been confirmed. Any names, numbers, or project descriptions seen in the current prototype are fabricated and must not be reused as fact.

## Product Principles
- Present only real, verifiable information about the owner — never carry forward placeholder content as if it were fact.
- Serve both recruiting and freelance-client audiences without designing exclusively for either.
- Treat the existing Figma-Make prototype as a structural/visual reference candidate, not as source-of-truth content.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
No product-specific requirement established.
